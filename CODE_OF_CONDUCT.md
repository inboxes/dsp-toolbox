# Code of conduct

This project is for anyone interested in audio and DSP, whatever their background or experience.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Keep criticism about the work, and make it useful.
- Help newcomers, and be patient with questions.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, personal attacks or insults.
- Discriminatory language or behaviour.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sexual content or unwelcome advances.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close discussions, or block people who break these rules.

To report a problem privately, use **Report a vulnerability** on the repository's **Security** tab and start the title with "Conduct:". It goes only to the maintainer.
